🗣️ Speaking - Phần thi nói
Mẹo: Cô giáo Cambridge rất hiền 🌷 Con cứ nói TO, RÕ, dám nói là được!
Phần 1: Chào hỏi
Hello! What's your name?
(Con tên gì?)
My name is Anh Thu.
How old are you?
(Con mấy tuổi?)
I'm eight years old.
How do you spell your name?
(Đánh vần tên đi)
A-N-H, T-H-U
Where are you from?
(Con đến từ đâu?)
I'm from Vietnam.
Phần 2: Chỉ tranh
Mẹo: Dùng giới từ in/on/under/next to
Where's the cat? 🐱
It's on the table. / It's under the chair.
What colour is it?
It's red / blue / yellow.
How many cats are there?
There are three.
What is the boy doing?
He's running. / He's eating.
Phần 3: Câu hỏi cá nhân
Do you like ice cream?
Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
What's your favourite animal?
My favourite animal is a rabbit. 🐰
What's your favourite colour?
My favourite colour is pink. 🌸
Have you got a sister?
Yes, I've got a sister. / No, I haven't.
What do you like to eat?
I like rice and chicken. / I like pizza.
Goodbye, Anh Thư!
Goodbye! Thank you!
